How HashRoll Works

1. Choose a future Bitcoin block
2. Wait until the block gets mined
3. Get the block hash from:

https://mempool.space

4. Convert the hash into a number
Character values: 0=0
1=1
2=2
...
9=9
A=10
B=11
C=12
D=13
E=14
F=15
Formula:
H = Σ(dᵢ × 16ⁱ)
Simple example:
Hash: 1A3 1=1 A=10 3=3 H=(1×16²)+(10×16¹)+(3×16⁰) H=256+160+3 H=419
5. Divide the converted number by the total amount of tickets
C = H ÷ T
6. Move through the wheel:
1 → 2 → 3 → ... → 50,000 → 1 → 2 ...
7. The ticket where the wheel stops becomes the winner
V=((C−1) mod T)+1

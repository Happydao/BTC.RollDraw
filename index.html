How HashRoll Works

1. Choose a future Bitcoin block
2. Wait until the block gets mined
3. Get block hash from:

https://mempool.space

4. Convert hash into number
H = Σ(dᵢ × 16ⁱ)
5. Divide:
C = H ÷ T
6. Move through wheel:
1 → 2 → 3 → ... → 50,000 → 1 → ...
7. Winner formula:
V=((C−1) mod T)+1

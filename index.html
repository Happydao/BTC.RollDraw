BTC HashRoll Verifier

Insert a Bitcoin block hash and total number of tickets. The system converts the hexadecimal hash into a number and calculates the winning ticket.

How it works


1. Bitcoin block hash
↓
2*. Convert the hash into a number
↓
3. Divide that number by the total amount of tickets
↓
4. The result determines how many steps the wheel moves
↓
5. The wheel counts:

1 → 2 → 3 → ... → 50,000 → 1 → 2 → ...

↓
6. The ticket where the wheel stops becomes the winning ticket


*Convert hash into a number:

A Bitcoin block hash is a sequence of numbers
and letters in hexadecimal format (base 16).

Character values:

0 = 0
1 = 1
2 = 2
...
9 = 9
A = 10
B = 11
C = 12
D = 13
E = 14
F = 15


Formula:

H = Σ (dᵢ × 16ⁱ)


Simple example:

Hash: 1A3

Values:

1 = 1
A = 10
3 = 3


Calculation:

H = (1×16²)
  + (10×16¹)
  + (3×16⁰)

H = 256
  + 160
  + 3

H = 419
